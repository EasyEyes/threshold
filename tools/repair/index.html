Repair EasyEyes results — nearest point wrong-coordinates bug

Between Aug 30 2025 and Sep 15 2026, EasyEyes read the tracked eye position in the wrong coordinate frame — placing it far up-and-right of its true position — and warped stimulus positions and sizes in distance-tracked sessions accordingly: mildly near the upper right, badly at the lower left. Fixation was always correct.

What the statuses mean

unaffected The bug did not distort these trials (e.g. the eye was at screen center, the session was untracked, or it was recorded outside the bug window).

corrected These trials were distorted. The requested values in the original columns are replaced with the true positions, sizes, and levels the participant saw, derived from the logged eye point and the same conversion code the app now uses.

flagged The file lacks the information needed to prove what was shown (hover a row for the specific reason). Nothing is invented — these rows are left for manual review.

How big is the error? There is no single formula: the app acted as if the eye were half a screen up-and-right of its true position, so the error depends on the screen's physical size, the viewing distance, the true eye position, and the stimulus location (smallest toward the upper right, worst at the lower left, nil at fixation).
